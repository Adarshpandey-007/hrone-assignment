Also note anything you looked at and decided was **not** a defect, and why:
- **Using `load_dotenv()` with `os.getenv()`:** By default in `python-dotenv`, `load_dotenv()` does not override pre-existing environment variables (`override=False`). Pre-existing system environment variables (`MONGO_URI`, `MONGO_DB`) take precedence over `.env`, satisfying the requirement "real environment variables must take priority".
- **Storing dates and shift times as strings:** Storing `date` as `"YYYY-MM-DD"` and `shift_start`/`shift_end` as `"HH:MM"` (IST) is intentional per `DATA_MODEL.md` to avoid timezone serialization ambiguities inherent in date-only types.
- **Addressing attendance records by `(emp_code, date)` without a surrogate primary key:** As explicitly detailed in the problem statement and data model, attendance records have no public ID; using natural compound key `(emp_code, date)` is by design.
